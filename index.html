    DOCUMENTATION & GAME AUDIO GUIDE

    Free Online Audio Bit-Crusher, Sample Rate Reducer & Game SFX Optimizer

    OGCruncher (often searched as OG Cruncher) is a browser-based creative DSP workstation built by Ihor Sivochka (Figarist). Drop WAV, MP3, FLAC, OGG, or AIFF files into the queue to crush bit depth, reduce sample rate, shape EQ/saturation, audition live Dry/Wet A/B differences, and batch-export game-ready audio entirely inside your browser via OfflineAudioContext and Float32Array Web Workers.

    1–16 Bit Quantization, Sample Rate Decimation & TPDF Dither

    Dial in authentic 4-bit, 8-bit, or 12-bit quantization alongside 4,000 Hz to 48,000 Hz sample rate decimation with instant hardware snap points. Toggle Crush Mode (DC offset removal, |x|^1.15 soft expander, and two-tap anti-alias smoothing), add TPDF triangular dither to soften quantization grain, drive harmonics with tanh Grit saturation, or layer tape-style Noise floor hiss.

    Simultaneous Batch Export: 8-Bit / 16-Bit PCM WAV, Ogg Vorbis & MP3

    Queue dozens of sound effects and crunch them sequentially under one locked parameter snapshot. Each job outputs three formats in parallel inside a dedicated Web Worker: unsigned 8-bit or signed 16-bit PCM WAV for zero-latency engine playback, Ogg Vorbis (.ogg) for compact Unity/Godot/WebGL loops, and 128 kbps MP3 (with windowed Lanczos-3 resampling for non-MPEG rates), plus one-click ogcruncher_batch.zip packaging.

    Retro Console Presets, Dry/Wet Spectrum Analyzer & 100% Local Privacy

    Switch between Simple quality tiers or Advanced rack presets (LO-Q MODE, NES 8-BIT, AMIGA 500, and custom saved presets). Compare Dry vs. Wet signals in real time with the FFT spectrum visualizer and RMS/Peak/Duration telemetry, copy shareable URL hash configurations, and work offline as an installable PWA—zero audio files ever leave your computer.

    Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

    What is OGCruncher and how does it differ from a standard audio compressor?

    OGCruncher is a free creative bit-crusher, sample rate reducer, and batch exporter built for indie game developers, Wear OS apps, and retro sound designers. Instead of transparent audio transcoding, it intentionally reshapes WAV, MP3, FLAC, OGG, and AIFF samples using OfflineAudioContext EQ/speed filters, soft expansion, 1–16 bit quantization, optional TPDF dither, and tanh saturation before exporting OGG, WAV, and MP3.

    Are my audio files uploaded to any external server?

    No. Your audio files stay on your device. Decoding, OfflineAudioContext filtering, Float32Array DSP processing, and OGG/WAV/MP3 encoding all run locally inside your browser and a dedicated Web Worker. Only static web assets and the optional demo track are fetched over HTTP.

    How does OGCruncher choose between 8-bit and 16-bit PCM WAV export?

    The WAV encoder automatically matches container precision to your Bit Depth setting: effect depths of 1 to 8 bits export as unsigned 8-bit PCM WAV, while depths of 9 to 16 bits export as signed 16-bit PCM WAV. That keeps zero-latency SFX files compact when targeting 8-bit crunch without discarding precision on 12-bit or 16-bit exports.

    Which export format and sample rate should I choose for Unity, Godot, WebGL, or Wear OS?

    Use 8-bit or 16-bit Mono PCM WAV at 16,000–22,050 Hz for ultra-short UI clicks, footsteps, and combat SFX that need instant playback without CPU decoding overhead. Use Ogg Vorbis (.ogg) at 22,050–32,000 Hz for ambient loops, music stems, and APK/WebGL asset budgets where file size matters most.

    Why are ultra-low sample rates (like 4,000 Hz) exported as 8,000 Hz in MP3?

    The MPEG audio standard used by MP3 supports nine discrete sample rates starting at 8,000 Hz (8k, 11.025k, 12k, 16k, 22.05k, 24k, 32k, 44.1k, and 48k Hz). When you select a non-standard rate such as 4,000 Hz or 28,000 Hz, WAV and OGG preserve your exact requested rate while the MP3 encoder resamples the crunched signal using windowed Lanczos-3 interpolation to the nearest valid MPEG rate.

    How do batch processing, ZIP export, and shareable preset links work?

    Drop multiple audio files into the queue, tune Simple or Advanced parameters, and click CRUNCH to freeze one validated snapshot across the entire batch. When processing finishes, download individual OGG, WAV, or MP3 files, grab everything at once via DOWNLOAD ALL (.ZIP), or click COPY LINK to share your exact DSP settings in the URL hash.
